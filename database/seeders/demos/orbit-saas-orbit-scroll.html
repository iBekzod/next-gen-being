Questions, answered.

Does Orbit change my other tools?

No. Connections are read-only by default. Orbit shows a unified view and never edits your data unless you turn on an automation.

How long does setup take?

Most teams connect their first four tools in about twelve minutes. This is a demo template, so nothing actually connects here.

Can I self-host?

Not in this fictional product. If you adapt the template, replace this answer with your own policy.

Is the pricing real?

No. Prices, figures and logos are placeholders for you to replace.
