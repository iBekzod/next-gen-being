    How a commission runs

    Six weeks, three fittings, no shortcuts.

    Week 0

    Consultation

    We talk about how you live and dress, take thirty-one measurements and choose cloth together.

    Week 2

    First fitting

    A basted jacket in your cloth. We mark, pin and re-cut whatever does not hang right.

    Week 4

    Second fitting

    Sleeves set, lapels rolled, pockets in. Small corrections only.

    Week 6

    Delivery

    Final press, buttons sewn, your monogram stitched inside. Free adjustments for life.
